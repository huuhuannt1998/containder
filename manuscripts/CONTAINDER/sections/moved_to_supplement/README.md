# Sections moved into the supplement

`03_formalism.tex` and `06_engine.tex` were moved into `supplementary.tex` as SM.5 and SM.6 to
bring the body within the IJCIP length recommendation. Neither supports a result: the body
disclaims the impact accounting as not a contribution, and the engine section documents a released
component that no physical result depends on.

The files are retained here because the supplement now holds edited copies (body cross-references
became prose, and the macros are declared locally). Do not add them back to `main.tex`; the body
no longer defines the `\BR*` macros they use.
