We do not claim that no graph model can represent time or physical impact. Temporal,
probabilistic, and attributed cyber-physical attack graphs
exist~\cite{poolsappasit2012dynamic,wang2008attackgraph,frigault2008measuring} and can encode
these quantities. Our claim is only that \emph{static, unweighted} reachability cannot compare DER
credential-containment policies, because it omits authorization scope, temporal validity, session
and command persistence, state-dependent flexibility, and feeder state.

Call a measurement \emph{reachability-only} if it is a function $M$ of the unchanged reachable set
$\Reach(x)$ alone. Three separations then follow at once. \emph{Scope:} hold the reachable DER set
fixed and vary only the scope map $A$ --- read-only versus control function sets --- and $\BRflex$
differs at equal reach. \emph{Time:} an attestation-gated ephemeral credential loses acceptance at
the next un-attested renewal while a long-lived unrevoked one does not, so $\BRauth$ and $\BRexp$
differ at equal initial reach. \emph{Physical state:} at fixed $x$ and $\pi$, the same authorized
command is harmless at one operating state and drives nodes past an IEEE~1547 overvoltage trip
threshold at another, and no privilege graph contains $u$ or $F$.

\textbf{These cannot fail, and we present them as definitions rather than findings.} $M$ is
single-valued in $\Reach(x)$, and each construction varies a quantity that is by definition not an
argument of $M$, so the propositions restate that definition. They say which quantities an
evaluation must move; they are not evidence that our design moves them. Each ships as a regression
test in the released engine so the notation is mechanically checkable. \textbf{The evidence for the
design is in Section~\ref{sec:prelim}, and only there.}

Two points of housekeeping. First, the third construction previously rested on a reverse-power
protection trip that the simulator could not represent, there being no protection model at all;
Section~\ref{sec:feeder} now implements the IEEE~1547-2018 Category~II overvoltage screen, and
Section~\ref{sec:prelim} reports the state dependence as a measured effect --- at light load with
high PV a given authorized reactive envelope trips in every seed, at normal load in none. Second,
an earlier version carried a worked counterexample of two compromises with identical reach but
different scope, lifetime, and feeder outcome. It illustrated the notation without separating
anything an experiment separates, so we have cut it; the same contrast now appears as measured
policy arms in Section~\ref{sec:prelim}. Extending a classical attack graph to distinguish such
cases requires resource-by-scope product nodes, a time index, and attached $u$ and $F$ --- which
is precisely the accounting of Section~\ref{sec:formalism}. That extension therefore agrees with
us, which is why we do not present the accounting as a theoretical advance.
